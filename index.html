THE MAKING OF A PIECE

From the first stitch
to the final detail.

Every garment is born at the intersection of artisanal precision and poetic silhouette. Over 40 hours of hand zardozi stitching, pure botanical dyeing, and hand-finished hems.

01

Thread Selection

Pure zari woven with 24k gold bullion leaf and high-twist Mulberry silk threads.

02

Artisanal Looming

Heritage pit-looms guided by generational master weavers in Varanasi and Chanderi.

03

Zardozi Needlework

Hand-guided needle embroidery placing intricate dabka, metallic coils, and seed pearls.

04

Bespoke Atelier Finishing

Hand-scalloped hems, steam-sculpted drape, and sealed with our master artisan's hallmark.
